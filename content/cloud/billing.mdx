# Billing

Ycode Cloud uses Stripe for subscription management and payments. Each project has its own subscription. Projects start on **Free** so you can build with the full editor. Upgrade per project when you are ready to host the site — there is no use case for running a website forever on the free plan.

## Free projects

Free projects are for building, not for hosting. Keep the project active in the editor (open it, edit, or publish). After **7 days of inactivity**, a never-subscribed Free project is deleted.

A published `*.ycode.website` site sitting idle still counts as inactive. If you want the site to stay online, [subscribe](#subscribing) to a paid plan.

Paid projects, and projects that used to be paid, are not deleted for inactivity.

<Callout type="warning" title="Deletion is permanent">
Idle Free projects are removed automatically after 7 days. There is no restore. Export a [backup](/docs/deployment/backup) before you step away, or upgrade to paid if the site should stay live.
</Callout>

## Subscribing

You subscribe, change plans, and pay invoices without leaving the builder. Checkout runs inside Ycode — there's no redirect to an external payment page.

On a Free Cloud project, the editor header shows a **Free** label and an **Upgrade** button after the locale selector and project URL. Every collaborator on the Free project sees this — not only people who can manage settings. Paid projects hide both.

<Steps>

### Pick a plan

Click **Upgrade** to open **Upgrade your project**. Switch between **Monthly** and **Annual** (annual plans carry a **Save yearly** badge), then click **Choose Basic** or **Choose Pro**.

| Plan | Annual (billed yearly) | Monthly | CMS collections | Languages |
|------|------------------------|---------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Basic** | $10/month | $15/month | 5 | 2 |
| **Pro** | $25/month | $30/month | 50 | Unlimited |

If the project already uses more than a plan allows, that plan is disabled with a note such as "This project exceeds the Basic plan: 8 CMS collections, limit is 5". Remove the extra collections or languages, or choose a larger plan.

### Enter billing details

Fill in **Full name or company**, **Country or region**, and **Address**. Turn on **I'm purchasing as a business** to add a **Tax ID type** and number. Once saved, the tax ID shows whether it was **Verified**.

### Add a payment method

Enter a card, or pick a card already saved on the project, then click **Continue to review**.

### Review and subscribe

The review shows **Billed to**, **Payment**, the plan price, any **Discount**, the **Tax** line, and **Due today**. Enter a **Promotion code** if you have one, then click **Subscribe**. If your bank asks for 3D Secure confirmation, approve it in the pop-up.

</Steps>

Once subscribed, the project gets access to all paid features including custom domains and publishing. See [Pricing](/docs/pricing) for everything included in each plan.

### Taxes

Plan prices exclude VAT or sales tax. Tax is calculated from your billing address and shown wherever a charge is previewed — at checkout, in the plan-change dialog, and on every invoice. EU businesses that add a valid VAT ID are invoiced with reverse charge instead of VAT.

## Managing a Subscription

Open the builder menu and choose **Billing**. Billing has its own sidebar:

| Page | What you can do |
|------|-----------------|
| **Plan** | See your plan, price, **Current period start**, and **Next invoice** date. Click **Change plan** to switch plans or intervals, or **Cancel plan** |
| **Invoices** | Every invoice from renewals, plan changes, and purchases, with its status (**Paid**, **Unpaid**, …). Click **View** or **Pay**, or download the PDF |
| **Payment methods** | Add a card, set the **Default** card used for renewals, or remove a card |
| **Billing details** | Set the **Billing email** that receives invoices and receipts, and the **Billing address & tax ID** printed on every upcoming invoice |

Only people who can manage the project's members can change billing; everyone else sees a read-only notice. Projects in an agency workspace show **Plan** and **Invoices** only, since the workspace handles payment.

### Changing Plans

Click **Change plan** on the **Plan** page, then **Switch to Basic** or **Switch to Pro**. Switching takes effect immediately and is prorated: the **Switch to** dialog shows the **Payment method**, the amount **Charged today**, and the **Next invoice** before you confirm.

### Cancelling

Click **Cancel plan** and confirm. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, and the **Plan** page shows a **Cancelling** badge with the **Ends on** date. After that, your custom domains are disconnected and the project reverts to the free plan. Click **Resume plan** any time before the end date to keep your subscription.

### Failed Payments

When a payment fails, Billing shows a **Your last payment failed** banner (or **You have an unpaid invoice**) with the outstanding amount. Click **Pay invoice** or **Update card** to keep your custom domains connected.

The **⋮** menu on a dashboard project card still has **Billing details**, which opens the Stripe Customer Portal for the same subscription.

<Callout type="info" title="Migrated from Legacy">
Ycode Legacy was shut down on July 1, 2026 and all active projects were migrated to the new Ycode. If you had a paid subscription on Legacy, it carried over automatically at your existing price with all features of the new Ycode unlocked.
</Callout>

## Project Status

Each project on the dashboard shows a category badge that reflects its billing state:

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Free** | The project is on the free tier with full editor features; custom domains are locked. Idle never-subscribed Free projects are deleted after 7 days |
| **Paid** | The project has an active subscription with custom domains and other paid features unlocked |
| **Lifetime** | A lifetime entitlement granted by Ycode (e.g. legacy migration with a permanent grant) |
| **Past due** | Payment failed; update the payment method to avoid interruption |
| **Cancelled** | The subscription ended; some features may be limited |
